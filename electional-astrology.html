The Art of Electional Astrology

What Is Electional Astrology?

Electional astrology is the traditional practice of choosing a favorable moment to begin something important, such as a wedding, a business launch, a contract signing, or a move. Rather than reading a chart after the fact, you look ahead for windows that match the guidelines for the kind of event you are planning.

The practice has roots in Mesopotamian omen traditions and was written down by Hellenistic authors such as Dorotheus of Sidon, whose Pentateuch (often called Carmen Astrologicum) dates to around the 1st century CE and is among the earliest surviving astrological texts. Tradition holds that Baghdad's founding in 762 CE was timed by court astrologers.

Electional Astrology by Event Type

💍
Best Date for a Wedding
Traditional guidance looks for a waxing Moon in Taurus, Cancer, or Libra, with Venus and Mercury direct. Friday, the day of Venus, is the classic choice. Avoid a void-of-course Moon at the ceremony.
🚀
Best Date to Launch a Business
Look for a waxing Moon with Mercury and Jupiter direct. Thursday (Jupiter) and Sunday (Sun) are the classic days. The Moon should not be void of course.
⚕
Surgery and Medical Care
Old texts prefer a waning Moon and warn against the Full Moon, but there is no evidence that astrology affects medical outcomes. Schedule care with your doctors, and never delay treatment because of astrology.
📜
Best Date to Sign a Contract
Mercury should be direct. Wednesday is Mercury's day. A waxing Moon in an earth sign suits long-term agreements. Read the terms carefully whatever the sky says.
✈
Best Date for Travel or Relocation
Mercury rules travel, so tradition prefers it direct. A Moon in a mutable or air sign suits movement, and the Moon should not be void of course at departure.
💰
Financial Decisions
Jupiter and Venus are the traditional allies. A waxing Moon in Taurus or Cancer is favored. This is symbolism for reflection, not financial advice.

Key Factors in Electional Timing

Moon Phase

A waxing Moon (new to full) is traditionally linked with growth and beginnings, and a waning Moon (full to new) with endings and release. The scanner computes the phase from the actual Sun-Moon angle.

Void-of-Course Moon

The Moon is void of course from its last exact major aspect to the Sun, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter or Saturn until it enters the next sign. Tradition says things begun then may come to nothing. The scanner calculates the real start and end times of each void period.

Retrogrades

Mercury retrograde is the best known: many astrologers avoid new contracts, launches, and travel then, and use the time for review. The scanner also checks Venus, Mars, and Jupiter where they matter for the event you chose.

Day Ruler

Each weekday belongs to a planet. Matching the day to the event, such as Friday for a wedding or Thursday for a launch, adds to a date's score. See the planetary hours calculator to choose a time of day.
